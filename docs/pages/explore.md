# Explore (`/explore`)

The community gallery: popular published art from public profiles.

**Who can open it:** everyone. Liking and publishing need an account.

## Features

### Gallery

- ✅ Popular tiles ranked by likes in a period: this week (default), month, year (`?period=`).
- ✅ Total count of published tiles.
- ✅ Cards: artwork, name, like button with count, artist avatar and name (links to their [profile](artist-profile.md)).
- ✅ Preview dialog: the full artwork, with animation playback when it has frames.
- ✅ Larger previews or compact grid.
- ✅ Loads 20 more as you scroll; "Couldn't load more tiles. Try again" if loading fails.
- ✅ Guests can browse the first page, then see a sign-up banner to keep scrolling.
- ✅ Only published art from public profiles appears.

### Header

- ✅ Publish to Explore: pick arts from your Pigxel cloud to publish (guests are asked to sign in).
- 🔨 Search ("Search projects, templates, assets…"): filters only the tiles already loaded, by name.
- 🔨 Sort: Popular, Recent, A-Z, Liked; sorts only the tiles already loaded.
- 🔨 Categories (All, Buildings, Characters, Fantasy, Environment, Icons): buttons shown, they don't filter anything yet.

### Likes

- ✅ Like and unlike (signed in); guests are sent to sign in.

## Planned and open

- 📋 Search and sort on the server, across all published art, not only what's loaded.
- ❓ Categories: tags chosen when publishing (same tag system as [Assets](assets.md)), or remove the buttons.
- ❓ Search also covering assets and templates, as the placeholder text promises, or change the text.
- 📋 Share previews: a link to an artwork shows its picture on social media and chat apps.
- ❓ "Following" feed: new art from artists you follow.
- ❓ Comments on artworks (out of scope today).
