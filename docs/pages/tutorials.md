# Tutorials (`/tutorials`, `/tutorials/[slug]`)

Lessons that teach pixel art in Pigxel, with a coach that guides you through the editor.

**Who can open it:** signed-in people.

## Tutorial list (`/tutorials`)

- ✅ Cards with picture, title, summary, level, length and number of steps.
- ✅ Five interactive lessons: Pixel art basics, Your first animation, Build a tileset, Shading and highlights, and Idle animation.
- ✅ Shows "Video soon" when a lesson has no video.

## Lesson (`/tutorials/[slug]`)

- ✅ Video, or a placeholder when there's none.
- ✅ Description, practice tile size and the ordered list of steps.
- ✅ **Start guide:** opens a fresh practice tile in the [Editor](editor.md) with the coach, without touching your other work.
- ✅ The coach points at the right tool, menu or panel and ticks steps off when you do them.
- ✅ Unknown lessons show [Page not found](not-found.md).
