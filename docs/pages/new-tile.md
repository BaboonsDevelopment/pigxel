# New tile (`/tiles/new`)

Sets up a new project before opening the editor.

**Who can open it:** signed-in people.

## Features

- ✅ Name (default "Untitled").
- ✅ Size: presets 16, 32, 64 and 128, or any width and height from 1 to 256.
- ✅ Background: transparent, white or black.
- ✅ Where to keep it:
  - **Pigxel cloud** (default): saved to your account and autosaved.
  - **Google Drive**: creates the file in your Drive and autosaves; offers to connect Drive if needed.
  - **Don't store it**: kept in this browser until you download or save it.
- ✅ Start from an asset (`?asset=`): the asset's picture, frames and timing become the new tile, as a new project of your own.
- ✅ Start with a palette (`?palette=`).
- ✅ Opens the new tile in the [Editor](editor.md), in a new tab without replacing other open work.
- ✅ Clear errors: invalid size, Drive not connected, or browser storage full (says whether the cloud or Drive copy was already saved).
