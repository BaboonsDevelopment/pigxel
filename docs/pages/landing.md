# Landing (`/`)

The public front door: shows what Pigxel is and turns visitors into sign-ups.

**Who can open it:** everyone. Signed-in people go straight to [Home](home.md).

## Features

### Hero and story

- ✅ Headline ("From the first doodle to finished game art"), sign-up and log-in buttons.
- ✅ Feature tabs: Draw, Animate, AI, Export, each with a short demo.
- ✅ Key numbers (biggest canvas, export scale, nothing to install) that count up.
- ✅ Inspiration wall of example pixel art.
- ✅ "Make a little something" interactive pixel playground: try drawing without an account.
- ✅ FAQ ("Answers").
- ✅ Footer: Product (Explore, Pricing, Sign up, Log in) and Legal links.

### Behind the scenes

- ✅ Skip link to the main content (keyboard and screen readers).
- ✅ Hands sign-in and password-reset links that arrive at `/` on to the right place.
- ✅ Playground drawings are never saved as projects.
