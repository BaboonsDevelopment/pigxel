# Artist profile (`/u/[username]`, `/profile`)

An artist's public page: who they are and the art they've published.

**Who can open it:** everyone for public profiles. Private profiles are visible only to their owner. `/profile` takes you to your own profile.

## Header

- ✅ Avatar, display name, `@username`, bio and up to three links.
- ✅ "Premium since …" badge for people on a paid plan; appears and disappears with the subscription.
- ✅ Joined date.
- ✅ Follower count.
- ✅ Follow / Following button for signed-in visitors; you can't follow yourself.
- ✅ Guests see "+ Follow", which sends them to sign up and back to this profile.
- ✅ Owner sees "Edit profile" and their AI credits.

## Arts

- ✅ Up to four pinned arts at the top.
- ✅ Published arts, newest first; the owner also sees private ones with publish and pin controls.
- ✅ Art count and an activity heatmap (days worked on arts over the last year).
- ✅ Only tiles kept in Pigxel cloud appear (not browser-only or Drive files); the owner sees an explanation.
- ✅ "Couldn't load these arts" when loading fails.

## Privacy

- ✅ A private profile shows "This profile is private" to others; the owner sees a notice with a link to change it.
- ✅ Guests can see public profiles, follower counts and activity, but not who follows whom, and can't change anything.
- ✅ Unknown usernames show [Page not found](not-found.md).
